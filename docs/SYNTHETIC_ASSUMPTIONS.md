# Synthetic Data Assumptions (upay ActivateAI)
- Generated by `backend/data_generator.py`, seed 42, 10,000 users. No real PII or upay data.
- Split: 8,000 train / 2,000 test, stratified by treatment x lifecycle_stage; zero customer_id overlap (enforced by pytest).
- Treatment is randomized ~50/50 (simulated RCT), independent of user features.
- Activation = 3+ solo-utility transactions within 30 days of offer delivery.
- Planted effects the model must rediscover (a limitation): Persuadables ~35% vs ~8% (+27pp); Sure Things ~40% vs ~40%; Lost Causes ~3% vs ~3%; Sleeping Dogs ~5% vs ~10% (-5pp).
- Offer costs (BDT): Bill Pay 30, Recharge 20, Super Shop QR 15, Toll/ATM 50.
- Wallet mix: Primary 50%, Salary 30%, Remittance 20%. Channel: 70% App, 30% USSD.
Full tables: see `docs/LOGIC_CHAIN.md` section 3.
